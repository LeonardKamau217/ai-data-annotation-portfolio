## Portfolio Projects

### 1. Personal Injury Law – AI Response Evaluation

Evaluated AI-generated responses to personal injury and premises liability scenarios.

**Evaluation criteria:**
- Relevance
- Factual accuracy
- Helpfulness

**Key activities:**
- Identified inaccurate legal reasoning and unsupported conclusions.
- Assessed whether AI responses addressed the scenarios appropriately.
- Documented evaluation decisions with written justifications.

### 2. Landscaping – AI Response Evaluation

Evaluated AI-generated gardening and landscaping recommendations.

**Evaluation criteria:**
- Relevance
- Factual accuracy
- Helpfulness

**Key activities:**
- Evaluated plant recommendations against sunlight requirements.
- Identified misleading advice about plant suitability and growing conditions.
- Assessed recommendations involving watering and shaded environments.
